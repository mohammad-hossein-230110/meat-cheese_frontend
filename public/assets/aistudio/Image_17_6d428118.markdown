## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a dark-first, cinematic culinary aesthetic tailored for bespoke, high-end gastronomic ordering. The experience speaks to discerning patrons seeking curated charcuterie, dry-aged cuts, and artisanal cheese selections. It evokes the sensory intimacy of an exclusive dimly lit cellar, tactile leather-bound menus, and ember-lit degustation spaces.

The visual direction merges **Minimalism** with **Low-Contrast Tonal Layering**:
- **Atmospheric & Subdued:** Depth is achieved through dark charcoal tonalities rather than heavy drop shadows, eliminating visual noise.
- **RTL-First Elegance:** Built from the ground up for right-to-left layout dynamics, pairing Persian typography with spacious margins and rhythmic vertical cadence.
- **Restrained Luxury:** Strict omission of gratuitous ornaments. Visual prestige relies entirely on precise hairline dividers, rich burgundy accents, and unhurried whitespace.

## Colors

The palette embraces deep carbon tones, rich burgundy reds reminiscent of vintage Bordeaux, and warm calcified whites.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#121010`):** The primary canvas background; an ultra-deep warm charcoal preventing the harshness of pure black (`#000000`).
- **Surface Level 1 (`#1c1817`):** Elevated cards, sheet drawers, and list row backgrounds.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#241f1e`):** Floating controls, active chips, stepper containers, and input fields.

### Brand Accents
- **Primary Interactive (`#6b0d0c`):** Vibrant deep burgundy designed to provide high-legibility touch targets against dark canvas layers.
- **Deep Wine (`#530201`):** Subdued brand base, utilized for pressed states, badges, and deep gradient underlays.
- **Accent Muted (`rgba(107, 13, 12, 0.18)`): Soft tinted wash for selected item highlights and active category pills.

### Content & Stroke Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#f7f5f2`):** Soft parchment white delivering readable contrast without ocular fatigue.
- **Text Secondary (`#a8a09b`):** Muted warm sand for dish descriptions, weights, and Persian numerals.
- **Text Tertiary / Disabled (`#7d7570`):** Subtle captions, dietary indicators, and inactive tab titles.
- **Hairline Borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`): Ultra-fine delineation preserving structural layout without visible compartmentalization.

## Typography

Typography is set up to support elegant Persian text layouts using rhythmic vertical metrics and clear proportional sizing.

### Typesetting & RTL Rules
- **Text Alignment:** Right-aligned by default across all layouts (`dir="rtl"`). Numeric values (prices, gram weights, curing aging times) follow Persian numeral conventions (`۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹`).
- **Line Heights:** Generous line heights are maintained (1.5–1.6× font size) to allow complex Persian ascenders, descenders, and tashkeel marks to breathe without overlap.
- **Weights:** Restricted to three weights: Regular (400) for descriptions, Medium (500) for subheads and badges, and SemiBold/Bold (600/700) for culinary names, pricing, and key calls-to-action.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for single-hand mobile interaction on modern viewport widths (390px baseline).

### Canvas Architecture
- **Fluid Single-Column Core:** Content flows strictly in a single column with a baseline screen margin of `1.25rem` (20px), maximizing thumb reach and reading comfort.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** 4px baseline sub-grid. Tight groupings (dish title + dietary tag) use `space-xs` (4px); medium pairs (title + price) use `space-sm` (8px); card internal padding leverages `space-md` (14px) to `space-lg` (20px).
- **RTL Fluidity:** Margins and paddings use logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`). Leading thumbnails sit on the physical right edge; price and quantity selectors anchor on the physical left edge.
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance:** An explicit floating clearance of `5.5rem` (88px) is enforced at the bottom of scroll containers to preserve safe scrolling above fixed order bars.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids traditional high-diffuse skeuomorphic drop shadows in favor of **low-contrast tonal depth** and **frosted translucency**.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (`#121010`):** Base layer supporting overall page scrolling.
2. **Structural Cards (`#1c1817`):** Elevated purely via background contrast and a microscopic top border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Sticky Nav:** `rgba(18, 16, 16, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle bottom hairline `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
4. **Primary CTA & Focus Nodes:** Elevated using a focused atmospheric burgundy glow rather than black shadows: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(107, 13, 12, 0.35)`.
5. **Interactive Pressed States:** Surfaces scale down minutely (`scale(0.985)`) with an immediate tint darkening, conveying physical resistance.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses subtle, soft-cornered geometry (level 1: 4px base radius) to evoke the clean lines of slate boards, marble counters, and butcher blocks.

- **Buttons & Primary Cards:** Base curvature is `0.5rem` (8px), avoiding overly pill-like bubbles in favor of tailored sophistication.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** Radii set to `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Dish Imagery:** Fitted with `0.5rem` (8px) borders, with optional unbordered full-bleed hero treatments.
- **Modals & Bottom Drawers:** Top edges curve at `0.75rem` (12px) to denote pull-up ergonomics without losing structural rigor.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action (Add to Order / Final Checkout):** Filled `#6b0d0c` with `#f7f5f2` SemiBold text. 52px height for generous mobile hit targets. Subtle top edge reflection via an inner hairline shadow (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Secondary Action (Customization / Wine Pairing):** `#1c1817` fill with hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` and `#f7f5f2` text.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No background, `#a8a09b` text, transforming to `#f7f5f2` on active tap.

### Dish & Product Cards
- **Architecture:** Unboxed or single-tier container strategy. Avoids nested borders within cards.
- **Horizontal Ordering Row (Default Menu Item):**
  - Right: Dish title in `headline-sm`, Persian weight/aging metadata (`body-sm`, `#a8a09b`), and brief provenance note.
  - Left: Clean numeric price (`label-lg`, `#f7f5f2`) directly above a minimal 36px plus/stepper button.
  - Optional Far-Right: 72×72px rounded thumbnail cropped tightly on the charcuterie/cheese texture.
- **Dividers:** Dishes separated by hairline rules: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.

### Category Navigation & Filter Chips
- **Format:** Horizontally scrolling pill strip (`dir="rtl"`) along the top with zero scrollbars.
- **Inactive:** `#1c1817` surface, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#a8a09b`.
- **Active:** `#6b0d0c` surface, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#f7f5f2`.

### Inputs & Quantity Steppers
- **Text Inputs (Special Instructions / Table Selection):** Dark surface `#1c1817`, text `#f7f5f2`, placeholder `#7d7570`. Hairline border turns to burgundy `#6b0d0c` upon focus.
- **Steppers:** Compact pill with `-` and `+` touch areas (40×40px min target), with Persian numeral count centered in bold text.

### Floating Cart Summary Bar
- **Positioning:** Fixed 16px above the viewport bottom, inset with `1.25rem` margin.
- **Style:** Deep charcoal glass container (`rgba(28, 24, 23, 0.94)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), perimeter bordered by hairline translucent white.
- **Layout:** Item count and total price in Persian typography on the right; "تکمیل سفارش" (Complete Order) CTA button on the left.
